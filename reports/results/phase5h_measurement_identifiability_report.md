# Phase 5H: Measurement-identifiability audit

This original-train-only descriptive audit evaluated 4 instrument-specific Memory composites and 6 concurrent instrument pairs. Original validation and test subjects were not loaded.

The selected Phase 5G state had graph-supported CCC **0.6296**, participant-bootstrap 95% CI **[0.6157, 0.6433]**. Among 6 pairs with an estimable reliability ceiling, the median attenuation ceiling was **0.7711** and 4 reached the reference 0.70 threshold.

Split-half coefficients quantify internal consistency of the available item subsets; 3--18 month coefficients mix reliability with true longitudinal change. The attenuation ceilings are therefore diagnostics rather than revised gates. Phase 5F is not reopened, and any threshold revision requires a separately frozen confirmation protocol.
